## Proposed migration sequence

1. **Confirm ownership and intent:** identify who owns each Medium, YouTube, Beehiiv, Suno, podcast, and social destination; decide whether it is an RBS asset, partner asset, or historical reference.
2. **Create a content ledger:** list each Medium article, YouTube video/playlist, podcast episode/feed, Suno track, newsletter landing page, and Shopify campaign/product link with original URL, rights/canonical status, audience, and recommended destination.
3. **Publish the first real local RBS entry:** keep the sample draft no-index; publish one owner-reviewed, source-backed Security/Bad Defaults piece under a permanent local URL.
4. **Establish video and newsletter rules:** approve the video-companion template/caption requirement and decide whether Beehiiv is retained, linked externally, or retired from primary promotion.
5. **Migrate selectively:** rewrite durable Medium pieces; add local video companions; create only editorially justified Goods context pages.
6. **Update internal links and UI:** replace legacy hash/deep external calls with approved routes and clearly labeled outbound links. Do not remove legacy code or third-party destinations until a production URL/crawl inventory and rollback plan exist.
7. **Archive and clean up:** retain an external-archive index as needed, mark retired campaigns, then remove unused feed components/assets in a separately authorized code cleanup.

## First real non-draft RBS content recommendation

**Recommendation:** publish an original, source-backed **Bad Defaults** explainer: **“Nobody Owns the Admin Account.”**

- **Why first:** it delivers immediate practical security value, introduces the RBS thesis without a services-company posture, and can be scoped safely without current-event dependence.
- **Format and destination:** `Security, Actually` / `Bad Defaults`; `/security/nobody-owns-the-admin-account`.
- **Required before publication:** owner byline, an approved source set (for example, authoritative identity/access documentation), clear scope/limitations, a practical ownership checklist, accessibility-ready media or no hero media, and factual/source review.
- **Do not:** copy a Medium article wholesale, name an unverified vendor/product, or use a product CTA unless a future Goods campaign has genuine contextual relevance and an approved disclosure.
