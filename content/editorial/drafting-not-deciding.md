## Drafting Is Not Deciding

AI can shorten the distance between a blank page and a plausible paragraph. That is occasionally useful. It is also a very efficient way to make an unexamined assumption look professionally formatted.

The useful distinction is small: drafting creates material; deciding accepts responsibility for it. The former can be accelerated. The latter still needs context, sources, and someone who knows what happens when the answer is wrong.

This is not a claim about a particular model or product. It is a draft editorial fixture used to test the RBS content model before any real article is published.
